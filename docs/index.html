Hien

A notepad that keeps up with you.

Hien is a small Windows notepad written in Rust. It opens fast, saves on its own and can find, replace or insert words across a note in one go.

Download for Windows Source code

Single .exe, no installer. Windows 10 or 11.

Features

Insert word

Put a word before or after every match. With cat and tabby, each cat becomes tabby cat. Matches that already have the word are skipped.

Find and replace

One at a time or all at once, with match case and whole word options. Matches are highlighted as you type.

Saves on its own

Notes are plain .txt files in your AppData folder, written 400 ms after you stop typing.

Undo and trash

Bulk changes undo with one button. Deleted notes move to a trash folder and come back from the status bar.

English and Turkish

The interface follows your system language. Turkish I and İ match their lowercase forms in search.

Dark and light

Follows the system theme, or pick one in Settings.
